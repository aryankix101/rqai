A — Shared Control

Capital-normalized results: 12.66% CAGR, 1.094 Sharpe, and -8.76% maximum drawdown.

B — De-prescribed Context

14.54% CAGR, 1.195 Sharpe, -8.40% drawdown. The +1.71-point lift came primarily from a +2.11-point short-side improvement; longs declined 0.40 points. Retain for its mechanism, not headline CAGR alone. Its CI crosses zero and trimmed lift is negative, so it is not validated.

C — Proportional Separation

11.64% CAGR, 1.002 Sharpe, -10.37% drawdown. It lost 0.90 points versus A, hurt both sides, achieved only 25% chronological-block stability, and is rejected.

D — B × C Interaction

D combined B's neutral context with: “Use the full 0–100 range. Two tickers that differ on a signal must receive distinct scores proportional to the signal's magnitude.”

It cached 137/251 dates through 2011-09-16 using 142 calls and 250,275 output tokens, then stopped on DeepSeek 402. No D return series was produced, so no CAGR or lift is claimed. Prune as an incomplete interaction-futility decision—not a statistical rejection—and preserve the cache.

Selection remains robustness- and mechanism-aware: short lift, long-side damage, stability, concentration, uncertainty, and weak-state behavior matter alongside CAGR. This preserves the earlier G0_02/G0_01 lesson that useful short-side improvement can matter even when headline CAGR is not the sole winner-selection key.

Executive Insights

Short version, with the dense evidence kept behind the linked pages.

Visible Model = Classifier

Ticker-visible scoring appears better at making the broad yes/no distinction: roughly deciding which half belongs in the book.

Anonymized Model = Ranker

Anonymized scoring appears better at ordering the highest-conviction ideas once the candidate pool is already technical.

Disagreement Is Directional

Generic disagreement penalties are too blunt. The interesting case is when the anonymized model ranks a stock high and ticker-visible does not.

Shorts Need A Different Job

The short book may be more useful as avoidance or hedge construction than as pure short alpha. Test that explicitly.

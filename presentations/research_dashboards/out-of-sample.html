How To Read The Results

Development 2009-2013: hypothesis generation and cache-based experiments, using dates from 2009-01-01 up to the exclusive 2014-01-01 endpoint. Out of sample 2015-2019: the separate five-year evaluation window, using dates from 2015-01-01 up to the exclusive 2020-01-01 endpoint.

Core harness: 60 long and 60 short candidates, 30/30 primary book, fixed five-trading-day C2C, zero costs, and 50/50 capital normalization.

Control design: A was collected once alongside B, then frozen and reused unchanged for C and D. The arms did not receive fresh controls.

A: The shared capital-normalized control produced 12.66% CAGR, 1.094 Sharpe, and -8.76% maximum drawdown.

B: Neutral/de-prescribed context produced 14.54% CAGR, 1.195 Sharpe, and -8.40% maximum drawdown. It improved the short book by 2.11 annualized percentage points while the long book declined 0.40 points. Its +1.71-point headline lift is promising but concentrated: the 95% interval crosses zero and its best-5%-trimmed lift is -1.60 points. Retain it as a development candidate, not a validated winner.

C: Forced use of the full 0-100 range with distinct proportional scores produced 11.64% CAGR, 1.002 Sharpe, and -10.37% maximum drawdown. It lost 0.90 annualized points versus A, damaged both sides, and was positive in only 25% of chronological blocks. Reject it.

D prompt: Combined B's neutral context with C's rule: Use the full 0-100 range. Two tickers that differ on a signal must receive distinct scores proportional to the signal's magnitude. Everything else remained fixed and tickers stayed anonymous. D produced 137/251 cached dates through 2011-09-16 (142 calls; 250,275 output tokens), then stopped on a DeepSeek 402 payment error. No portfolio-return result was produced, so no interim CAGR or lift is claimed.

D decision: Prune for interaction futility because its only additional proposition is that B rescues C's already harmful rule. Preserve the cache and label D incomplete—not statistically rejected. No additive effect was assumed, and the blind period remains untouched.

Strongest evidence supporting the thesis

The LLM beat the same-information nonlinear tabular model by 3.42 annualized percentage points on their common past-only evaluation window; the strongest weakest-factor-quintile result was 3.02 points for 20260806 G0_02 expected-magnitude scoring.

Strongest evidence against the thesis

Every tested prompt has a 95% paired-lift interval crossing zero and negative lift after removing its best 5% of dates; the apparent edge is concentrated and remains model/prompt dependent.

Required before blind validation

  • Predeclared B/C/D runs against the single shared fresh A control, or their paired analysis, are incomplete.
  • No shortlisted prompt retains positive lift after removing its best 5% of dates.
  • No shortlisted prompt has a full-development 95% paired-lift lower bound above zero.
  • Weak-state lift does not yet have a 95% interval entirely above zero.
